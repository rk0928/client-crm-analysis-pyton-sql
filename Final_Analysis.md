# Final Analysis

## Executive Summary

A CRM dataset containing 325 client records was analyzed using Python and SQL.

The dataset was cleaned in Python using Pandas and imported into MySQL for analysis.

---

## Active vs Dormant Clients

| Status | Count |
|----------|----------|
| Active | 226 |
| Dormant | 99 |

Active clients account for the majority of the customer base.

---

## Dormant Client Percentage

Dormant Clients: 99

Total Clients: 325

Dormant Percentage: 30.46%

This indicates approximately one-third of clients may benefit from re-engagement campaigns.

---

## Client Growth Analysis

Client acquisition increased significantly over the last three years.

| Year | Clients Added |
|------|------|
| 2024 | 19 |
| 2025 | 21 |
| 2026 | 205 |

2026 accounted for 63.08% of all client records in the database.

---

## Most Recent Client Activity

Recent client additions occurred throughout May 2026, indicating continued growth and successful customer acquisition efforts.

---

## Business Recommendations

1. Develop a dormant client outreach campaign.
2. Continue acquisition strategies used during 2026.
3. Track client engagement metrics monthly.
4. Create automated follow-up reminders for inactive clients.
5. Monitor retention rates alongside future growth metrics.

---

## Conclusion

The CRM database demonstrates strong recent growth, with most clients added during 2026. While the majority of clients remain active, approximately 30% are dormant and represent an opportunity for future customer retention efforts.
